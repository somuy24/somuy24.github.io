Remitly · UAE launchBuilt from scratch

Regulatory reporting service

Under its UAE licence, Remitly must report every remittance to the Central Bank each day in a strict, regulator-defined format, and there was no service for it. I created one and built its core: a country-agnostic microservice that consumes transaction events, enriches each one with customer, identity, recipient and payment data from upstream services, validates it against the regulator's spec and stores it for the daily file. Every step is idempotent and retry-safe, so a replayed or duplicate event never produces a wrong report row, and a lookup cache I added skips 80% of upstream calls in production.
